# Deploy freeze

The weekly deploy freeze runs from Friday 12:00 to Monday 09:00. Urgent fixes during the freeze need approval from the on-duty lead. Changed after a Saturday outage caused by a Friday-evening deploy. This replaces [[Deploy freeze (old)]].
